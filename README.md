
# AI-Driven Research Framework

> A structured framework for AI-assisted business research, source verification, evidence tracking, and responsible evidence-based decision-making.

## Overview

The **AI-Driven Research Framework** demonstrates how Generative AI can support different stages of business research while maintaining research integrity, source credibility, and human oversight.

This project focuses on converting broad topics into focused research questions, developing research plans, identifying credible sources, verifying AI-generated claims, and producing evidence-based business insights.

> **AI can assist research, but original evidence must support the final conclusions.**

---

## Objectives

- Understand the fundamentals of AI-assisted research.
- Convert broad business topics into focused research questions.
- Use AI to generate research subtopics and search keywords.
- Identify and evaluate primary and secondary sources.
- Verify AI-generated statistics, claims, and citations.
- Build structured evidence tables.
- Apply source triangulation to important claims.
- Separate facts, interpretations, and recommendations.
- Develop research reports with appropriate citations.
- Promote responsible and ethical use of AI in research.

## Core Research Framework

### 1. Research Question Development

A broad topic should be converted into a focused research question.

**Broad Topic:**

```text
AI in Retail
```

**Focused Research Question:**

```text
How is Generative AI changing personalized customer experiences in online retail?
```

A focused research question helps define the research scope and identify relevant evidence.

---

### 2. AI-Assisted Research Planning

AI can assist researchers with:

- Generating research subtopics
- Creating search keywords
- Identifying relevant source types
- Organizing research findings
- Identifying information gaps
- Drafting research structures

AI-generated information must be checked against original sources before being treated as evidence.

---

### 3. Source Evaluation

Sources are evaluated using the following criteria:

| Criteria | Evaluation Focus |
|----------|------------------|
| Authority | Who created or published the source? |
| Currency | Is the information sufficiently recent? |
| Evidence | Does the source provide supporting data? |
| Relevance | Does it address the research question? |
| Verification | Can important claims be confirmed? |

### Primary Sources

Examples include:

- Government reports
- Company annual reports
- Official datasets
- Original research studies
- Regulatory publications

### Secondary Sources

Examples include:

- News articles
- Industry analysis
- Review articles
- Business publications

## Claim Verification Process

AI-generated claims and statistics should be verified before inclusion in a research report.

### Verification Steps

1. Identify the AI-generated claim.
2. Ask AI for the original source.
3. Locate the original publication.
4. Open and read the source.
5. Search for the specific claim or statistic.
6. Check the year, context, and methodology.
7. Determine the verification status.
8. Record the findings in the evidence table.

### Verification Status

- **Verified:** The original source supports the claim.
- **Partially Verified:** The source supports only part of the claim or lacks relevant context.
- **Not Verified:** The claim cannot be confirmed using the available evidence.

> If a claim cannot be verified, it should not be presented as an established fact.

## Evidence Table

An evidence table helps organize and evaluate research claims.

| Research Claim | Source | Publication Date | Verified? | Evidence/Notes | Use in Report? |
|----------------|--------|------------------|-----------|----------------|----------------|
| Research claim | Source reference | Date | Yes/No | Supporting evidence | Yes/No |

The evidence table should contain at least five research claims for the practical activity.

---

## Fact, Interpretation, and Recommendation

Research reports should clearly distinguish between evidence and analysis.

### Fact

A statement supported by reliable evidence.

```text
Company sales increased by 12% in 2025.
```

### Interpretation

An analysis of what the evidence may indicate.

```text
This growth may indicate stronger customer demand.
```

### Recommendation

A suggested action based on the evidence and interpretation.

```text
The company should investigate expansion into similar markets.
```

### Research Structure

```text
Evidence → Interpretation → Recommendation
```

---

## Source Triangulation

Triangulation involves comparing information from multiple reliable sources to improve confidence in an important claim.

Potential sources may include:

- Government or regulatory information
- Company annual reports
- Academic research
- Established industry reports

Differences between sources should be examined by considering their publication dates, methodologies, definitions, and limitations.

## Research Integrity Principles

This project follows the following principles:

- Do not invent sources or data.
- Do not use fake citations.
- Verify AI-generated claims independently.
- Do not present unverified information as fact.
- Cite original sources appropriately.
- Do not copy research reports without proper attribution.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Avoid uploading confidential information without permission.
- Maintain responsibility for the accuracy of the final report.

## What AI Can and Cannot Do

| AI Can Help With | Researcher Must Do |
|------------------|--------------------|
| Brainstorm research questions | Select a meaningful research question |
| Suggest keywords | Find actual sources |
| Summarize information | Check summaries against original sources |
| Organize findings | Decide what information is relevant |
| Compare information | Verify important claims |
| Draft report structures | Develop the final analysis |
| Improve writing | Take responsibility for the final submission |

## Limitations

- AI-generated content may contain inaccurate or unsupported information.
- AI may produce fabricated sources, citations, or statistics.
- Source credibility depends on the quality and context of the evidence.
- Research findings may become outdated over time.
- AI summaries should be checked against original publications.
- Final conclusions require human review and judgment.
